# Dr. Copilot — real-life use cases, and what the doctor asks

---

## Before the patient sits down

### Thirty-second brief
- Brief me on this patient
- What do I need to know before I go in?
- Summarise this patient
- Give me the last three visits in short
- Why is she here today?

### Covering another doctor's patient
- What happened at the last visit?
- Who has been seeing her?
- What was the plan?
- What is she on right now?
- Show me the last prescription

### Late follow-up
- When did she last visit?
- How long since I last saw her?
- What did I ask her to come back for?
- Is her follow-up overdue?
- How many times has she come this year?

---

## During the consultation

### The patient asks what was given last time
- What was prescribed at the last visit?
- What did I give her last time?
- Which brand did I prescribe?
- What dose was it?
- For how many days?

### Considering a dose change
- Has the dose changed?
- How long has she been on this?
- Is she still on the same dose as last time?
- When did I start this drug?
- Has she ever been on a higher dose?

### Avoiding a duplicate order
- What investigations did I advise?
- Did I already order a CBC?
- Which tests are pending?
- When was her last CBC?
- Did she do the tests I asked for?

### Chronic review
- What changed across recent records?
- What is different from last time?
- Anything new since her last visit?
- What has stayed the same?
- Compare this visit with the previous one

### Teleconsult
- Summarise this patient
- What is she on right now?
- What were her last vitals?
- What was advised last time?
- Any allergy on file?

---

## At the prescribing moment

### Allergy and reaction check
- Any drug reaction documented?
- Is she allergic to penicillin?
- Any allergies on file?
- Did she react to anything I gave her?
- Has she had this drug before?

### Repeat prescription
- What is she on right now?
- What medications were recorded?
- What was prescribed at the last visit?
- Is anything due to be stopped?
- Repeat the last prescription

### Recording today's diagnosis
- What conditions are recorded?
- What is she being treated for?
- Since when has she had this?
- What is her ICD code?
- Any comorbidities?

---

## Between and after visits

### A report has arrived
- Has she uploaded anything recently?
- What reports are on file?
- What does the last report say?
- When was that report taken?
- What did I advise before this report?

### Certificate or insurance form
- When did she visit?
- What was the diagnosis?
- Any procedure on record?
- How many visits this year?
- What dates was she treated?

### Referral letter
- Summarise this patient
- What is the problem list?
- What has she been on, and for how long?
- What investigations have been done?
- What was the last advice given?

### Medico-legal lookup
- What exactly did I write on 24 April?
- Show me that visit
- What was prescribed that day?
- What was recorded as the diagnosis?
- Who saw her that day?

---

## Vitals and measurements

### Checking today's numbers
- BP today?
- What was the latest recorded blood pressure?
- Pulse and SpO2 last time?
- Show me all vitals from today
- What was her temperature at the last visit?

### Tracking over time
- What is her weight trend?
- Has she gained weight since last visit?
- Show me her BP across visits
- Any fever recorded recently?
- When was the last time vitals were taken?

---

## Labs and results

### Reading results
- What lab results are on file?
- What is her latest HbA1c?
- Show me the HbA1c trend
- Any abnormal labs?
- When was her last blood test?

### Following up on results
- Which tests are pending?
- Did she do the tests I asked for?
- What did the last report say?
- Has anything come back since the last visit?
- What investigations were advised?

---

## History

### Background
- Does she smoke?
- Any family history of diabetes?
- What is her occupation?
- Any surgery on record?
- Any relevant social history?

### Specialty
- What does the obstetric history show?
- What was her last menstrual period?
- Which vaccines are due?
- What is her visual acuity?
- What does the gynec history show?
